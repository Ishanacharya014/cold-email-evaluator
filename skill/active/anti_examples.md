# Cold Email Anti-Examples

These anti-examples are intentionally weak reference cases scored against the 5-point rubric:
- Relevant
- Specific
- Clear
- Human
- Low friction

They show what bad cold outreach looks like so the evaluator can recognize and penalize it.

---

## Weak Example 1

### Subject
Quick note

### Body
Hey,

I help businesses grow through AI-powered marketing solutions.

I would love to connect and discuss how we can work together.

Please let me know if you have time this week.

Best,
John

### Expected Scores
- Relevant: 1
- Specific: 1
- Clear: 3
- Human: 2
- Low friction: 2

### Why this is weak
- The subject line is generic and forgettable.
- The message could be sent to almost anyone.
- There is no specific reason why this person was chosen.
- The offer is vague and sounds mass-sent.
- The ask is soft and unbounded.

## Weak Example 2

### Subject
Checking in

### Body
Hi there,

I came across your profile and thought I would reach out.

We have a platform that helps teams streamline workflow and improve efficiency.

Would you be open to learning more?

Thanks,
Ryan

### Expected Scores
- Relevant: 1
- Specific: 1
- Clear: 2
- Human: 2
- Low friction: 1

### Why this is weak
- It does not show any real research.
- The opener is impersonal.
- The value proposition is generic.
- The ask is vague and too open-ended.
- It feels like a broadcast message.

## Weak Example 3

### Subject
Amazing opportunity

### Body
Hi,

I believe your company could benefit from our revolutionary solution.

We help modern brands unlock next-level growth with cutting-edge AI.

Let me know if you are interested and I can share more details.

Best,
Mia

### Expected Scores
- Relevant: 1
- Specific: 1
- Clear: 2
- Human: 1
- Low friction: 2

### Why this is weak
- The subject line is hype-heavy and meaningless.
- The copy uses buzzwords instead of concrete facts.
- There is no personalized context.
- The language sounds artificial and salesy.
- The ask is vague and requires too much effort.

## Weak Example 4

### Subject
No pitch here

### Body
Hi Alex,

I hope you are doing well.

I just wanted to reach out and see if you might maybe have a few minutes sometime to talk about something that could be helpful.

Please let me know if you are open to a conversation.

Best,
Sam

### Expected Scores
- Relevant: 1
- Specific: 1
- Clear: 1
- Human: 2
- Low friction: 1

### Why this is weak
- It hides the ask instead of stating it.
- It uses hedging and vague language.
- It gives no concrete reason for reaching out.
- It creates confusion instead of clarity.
- It asks for a conversation without explaining why.

---

## Patterns to Penalize

Bad cold emails usually:
- use generic subject lines
- fail to explain why this person was chosen
- sound copy-pasted
- rely on buzzwords instead of specifics
- hide the ask
- use hedging
- feel like mass outreach
- create too much friction to reply
- sound overly formal or AI-generated

## Patterns to Flag Strongly

The evaluator should strongly penalize:
- “Quick note”
- “Checking in”
- “No pitch here”
- “Amazing opportunity”
- “I would love to connect”
- “Let me know if you are interested”
- vague offers
- missing personalization
- no clear next step
